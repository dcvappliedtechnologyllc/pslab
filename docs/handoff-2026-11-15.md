# pslab handoff: gaps found in the 2026-11-15 session

Source: one long working session on `IMG_8611.JPG` (3213 x 4284) and `IMG_8611new1-1.psd` (2450 x 4356, 9:16). Work covered lighting looks, a two-line title with flanking bars, text shadows, moving and centering the title block, and removing two ceiling lights.

Every item below is something the session needed and pslab could not do directly, or did wrongly. Each has what happened, the proposed tool, and the Photoshop steps to record. All coordinates are image pixels.

For each new tool: record the descriptor with `dev_events` while doing the listed steps by hand, then follow `docs/research/uxp-api-notes.md` and `plugin/lib/recorded/README.md`.

## 1. Bug: levels layer with a polygon mask stops being masked

**What happened.** To draw two white bars I added two `levels` layers with `rgb: {out_black: 255, out_white: 255}` (everything to white) and confined each with `mask_from_polygon` (a 260 x 8 px rectangle, `feather_px: 0`). `render_preview` straight after showed two correct bars. A minute later the user reported the whole screen white. `render_preview` confirmed an all-white composite with only a faint outline of the right bar. `preview_mask` on both layers still showed the correct masks (black with a small white bar). Hiding the two layers with `compare_preview layer_ids_off` restored the photo.

**Unknown.** Whether the user touched anything in between. No pslab call ran between the good render and the bad one.

**To investigate.**
- After `mask_from_polygon`, read the layer's `userMaskEnabled`, `userMaskLinked`, mask density and feather, and compare with `preview_mask` output. `preview_mask` may be reading the channel while the mask is disabled.
- Check whether the mask is created through a selection that stays active, and whether a later deselect or a user click changes how the layer composites.
- Repro: open any JPG, `add_adjustment type:"levels" params:{rgb:{out_black:255,out_white:255}} above:<text layer>`, `mask_from_polygon` small rectangle, add a second one above it the same way, then click around the Layers panel in Photoshop and re-render.

**Until fixed.** `get_document` should report `maskEnabled` per layer so a disabled mask is visible to the caller.

## 2. `pslab: add move_layer`

**What happened.** "Move text and bars down a bit", "another 100", "down 100", "move it the 50". No tool moves a layer. First two rounds: screen control, clicking each layer in the Layers panel and sending `shift+down` 12 and 10 times per layer (96 keystrokes, 96 "Nudge" history states). Later rounds: `delete_layer` then `copy_to` again at a new `cy`.

**Tool.** `move_layer { doc?, layers: [id...], dx, dy }` and/or `{ x, y }` for absolute top-left. Multiple ids move together. One history state named "Move layers".

**Photoshop steps to record.** Select layer, Edit > Free Transform, set X/Y in the options bar, commit. Or the `move` descriptor with `offset` horizontal/vertical on a `layer` target.

## 3. `pslab: add reorder_layer`, and `above` on all layer-creating tools

**What happened.** `copy_to`, `clone_from` and `content_aware_fill` have no placement control. `copy_to` lands above whatever is active; the fill and clone tools land on top of the stack. Consequences:
- Text copied into the 9:16 doc landed under the Vignette layer, so the vignette dimmed the lettering. Fix was deleting and re-adding the vignette.
- Ceiling-light patches would have landed above the text and above the lighting layers. Fix was deleting all 7 layers, patching the bare Background, then rebuilding 3 adjustments, a mask and 4 text/bar layers.

**Tools.**
- `reorder_layer { doc?, layer, above? | below? | to: "top" | "bottom" }`.
- Add `above` to `copy_to`, `clone_from`, `content_aware_fill`, `add_pixel_layer` (same meaning as on `add_adjustment`).

**Photoshop steps to record.** Layer > Arrange, and dragging a layer in the panel (`move` descriptor with `to: { _ref: "layer", _index }`).

## 4. `pslab: add add_shape`

**What happened.** "Add two small horizontal bars." No way to create filled pixels. Attempt 1 was the levels-plus-mask hack that triggered bug 1. Attempt 2 exploited the white-out: `clone_from` copied an 8 px strip of the (accidentally white) composite into two pixel layers.

**Tool.** `add_shape { doc?, kind: "rect" | "line" | "ellipse", bbox | from+to+thickness, color: {r,g,b}, opacity?, name, above? }`. A shape layer is better than pixels: it stays editable and scales cleanly.

**Photoshop steps to record.** Rectangle tool with a fixed size, fill colour, no stroke. Or Layer > New Fill Layer > Solid Color with a vector mask.

## 5. `pslab: add add_layer_style`

**What happened.** "Add a faint black shadow to text and bars." No layer-style tool, and the Layer Style dialog cannot be driven in the background. Each shadow was built as: `copy_to` a duplicate offset 6 to 7 px down, `blur_layer` (10 px text, 6 px bars), `add_adjustment type:"levels" clip_to` with `out_black:0, out_white:0` to turn it black, `set_layer opacity`. Four shadows took 14 calls and added 8 layers. They do not follow the text if it is edited or moved, and the bar shadows cannot go darker than 100% opacity.

**Tool.** `add_layer_style { doc?, layer, style: "drop_shadow", params: { color, opacity, angle, distance_px, spread_pct, size_px } }`, plus `set_layer_style` and `clear_layer_style`. Stroke and outer glow are the obvious next two.

**Photoshop steps to record.** Layer > Layer Style > Drop Shadow on a text layer and on a pixel layer (`set` with `layerEffects` / `dropShadow`).

## 6. `pslab: add align_layers`

**What happened.** "The text needs center." Done with screen control: Select > All Layers, Select > All, Layer > Align Layers to Selection > Horizontal Centers, Select > Deselect. It worked only because every other layer was already full-canvas; with offset layers it would have moved them too.

**Tool.** `align_layers { doc?, layers: [id...], to: "canvas" | "selection" | <layer id>, horizontal?: "left" | "center" | "right", vertical?: "top" | "center" | "bottom" }`. Also `distribute`.

**Photoshop steps to record.** Select the target layers, Select > All, each of the six Layer > Align Layers to Selection items.

## 7. Layer bounds in `get_document`

**What happened.** No way to read where a text layer sits. Positions were measured off `render_preview` images and multiplied by `scale`. Only `copy_to` returns bounds.

**Change.** Add `bounds { left, top, right, bottom }` to every layer in the `get_document` tree. For text layers also add `text { content, font, style, size_pt, color, justification }`.

## 8. `pslab: add select_layer`

**What happened.** `copy_to` pastes above the active layer, and nothing sets the active layer. `set_layer` leaves `activeLayerId: null`. `set_adjustment` happens to activate its layer, so I called it with unchanged values purely to steer where the next paste landed.

**Tool.** `select_layer { doc?, layers: [id...] }`. Largely unnecessary once item 3's `above` exists.

## 9. Suspected bug: `render_preview` with a region returns the wrong area

**What happened.** On doc 226, `render_preview region:{left:200, top:450, right:2250, bottom:1150} max_px:1025` reported `scale: 2`. Mapping preview pixels back with that region put the title at y 790 to 906. Its real bounds from `copy_to` were y 567 to 681, and a full-canvas `render_preview` agreed with the real bounds. Working backwards, the returned image matches a region starting near (94, 277), not (200, 450). A second region call at exactly `scale: 2` also looked offset. Region calls at non-integer scales (1.54, 2.01, 2.47) were consistent.

**Status.** Two observations, not reproduced deliberately. Possible link to exact integer scale factors.

**Repro to try.** Put a pixel layer with known bounds on a document, request a region whose width is exactly 2x `max_px`, and compare.

## 10. Bug: `clone_from` fails when `src_bbox` is the whole canvas

**What happened.** `clone_from src_bbox:{0,0,3213,4284} dst:{0,0}` returned `BATCHPLAY: batchPlay step 2 (delete):` with no message. Insetting the box by 2 px on each side worked. Likely the inverse selection is empty, so the delete step has nothing to act on.

**Fix.** Skip the inverse-delete when the box covers the canvas. Better: add `stamp_visible { name, above? }` for this case (it is the first step of any glow effect).

## 11. `content_aware_fill`: control what is sampled and where the patch lands

**What happened.** The fill stamps the visible composite, so text over the area is baked into the patch and adjustment layers are baked in too. The patch then sits on top, outside the adjustments, so any later lighting change leaves two mismatched squares.

**Change.** Add `sample: "visible" | "below" | [layer ids]` and `above`. Default for interior work should be: sample the Background plus existing patch layers, place directly above them.

## 12. `pslab: add transform_layer`

**What happened.** The title had to be 76% of its original size on the narrower canvas. Only `copy_to` can scale, so changing size or position means deleting the layer and copying it again from the other document.

**Tool.** `transform_layer { doc?, layer, scale_pct?, rotate_deg?, cx?, cy? }`.

## 13. Batch operations

- `delete_layer` should accept `layers: [id...]`. Two rebuilds needed 7 sequential deletes each because parallel deletes are not safe.
- A `batch` tool that runs a list of pslab operations inside one `suspendHistory` block would turn an 18-call rebuild into one call and one undo step.

## 14. `pslab: add match_look`

**What happened.** "Fix the photo to match the lighting from Edits." There is no way to measure a reference. I compared video frames by eye and said so.

**Tool.** `match_look { doc?, reference_path | reference_doc, strength? }`: read mean RGB and a luminance histogram from the reference (a screenshot or exported frame), compare with the document, and add a Levels/Curves pair that closes the gap. Return the measured before, reference and after values. `match_tone` already does this for one layer against a region; this is the whole-image version.

## 15. Look recipes as single tools

Both were built by hand more than once this session.

- `add_glow { radius_px, opacity, blend }`: stamp visible, Gaussian blur as a smart filter, blend mode, opacity. Default blend should be `soft_light`. `screen` lifted every dark tone and the user called the result "too much blur fade" and "too washed out".
- `add_vignette { amount, center?, radius_px? }`: negative-exposure Light layer with an inverted radial gradient mask.

## 16. Report modal state

**What happened.** While the user had a crop in progress, `get_document` returned a tree with only "Crop Preview" and "Background" and a width of 3216, and my adjustment layer ids came back `NO_LAYER`. Only `render_preview` returned `MODAL_BUSY`.

**Change.** `get_document` should return `modal: "crop" | "transform" | "text" | null`, and layer tools should return `MODAL_BUSY` instead of `NO_LAYER` while a modal tool is active.

## 17. Editable blur

`blur_layer` cannot be re-run to change the radius (it would stack a second filter). Add `set_blur { layer, radius_px }` that edits the existing smart filter.

## Notes for the interior-photo-edit skill

Lessons from this session worth adding to the skill text:

- **"Dreamy" has a narrow target.** The sequence that was rejected: plain brighten ("hates it, much dreamier"), then Clarity -40 with a Screen glow at 35% ("too much blur fade"), then Screen glow at 15% with lifted blacks ("too washed out"). What was accepted: Exposure +0.55, Contrast +10, Highlights -55, Shadows +35, Blacks -8; Clarity -20; Vibrance +22, Saturation +6; glow on Soft Light at 32%. Rule: never lift blacks or use Screen for a dreamy look; keep contrast positive and get softness from negative clarity plus a Soft Light glow.
- **"Warm and luxurious"** accepted recipe on a bright source: Curves rgb `[[0,0],[60,66],[128,162],[195,227],[255,255]]`, red mid 128 to 133, blue mid 128 to 121 and white 255 to 250; Vibrance +14, Saturation +4; vignette -0.2 stops, radial, inverted. First attempt (rgb mid 130, red 136, blue 118, vignette -0.55) was too dark and yellow.
- **Never draw shapes with an adjustment layer and a mask** until bug 1 is fixed.
- **Before `content_aware_fill`:** text and adjustment layers must not be visible, and the patch must end up under the lighting stack. Until item 11 lands, patch the bare Background first, then build everything else.
- **Before placing a title:** find ceiling lights and other bright spots in the title zone and either place around them or offer to remove them. Two rounds were lost to a title sitting on a recessed light.
- **Text over a pale ceiling loses contrast each time the photo is brightened.** Offer a shadow as soon as text is added.
- **Order of work when text is involved:** cleanup patches, then lighting, then text and shapes, then shadows. Lighting layers go under the text so white type stays white.
- **Instagram safe zone:** on a 9:16 frame, flag any title whose top is within the top ~12% of the height.
